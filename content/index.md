# Kepos

A community-maintained collection of graduate program experiences —
syllabi, course notes, reading lists, and application experiences — aimed
primarily at Iranian students considering or currently in Master's/PhD
programs abroad.

Named for Epicurus's *Kepos* ("The Garden"), a school unusually open to
outsiders for its time. The goal here is similar: a space that grows as
contributors from different programs and universities add their own
experience.

## Sections

- **Universities** — program overviews, course notes, and session-by-session
  content, organized by university → semester → course.
- **Application experiences** — first-person accounts of applying to
  programs abroad.
- **Reading lists** — shared reading lists across programs and fields.
