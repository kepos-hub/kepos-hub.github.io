# Session 2 (Apr 17): What Is the Good Life? The Hedonist's Picture

## Required reading

- Shafer-Landau, *The Ethical Life*, Introduction.
- Epicurus, *Letter to Menoeceus*.
- Mill, John Stuart, *Hedonism* (course textbook, ch. 1).

## Discussion focus

Epicurus's case against fearing death and his picture of the most
pleasant life; Mill's claim that pleasure and freedom from pain are the
only things desirable as ends; whether either author's account of a good
life is plausible.
