# Session 4 (May 8): Divine Command Theory

## Required reading

- Plato, *Euthyphro* (course textbook, ch. 6).

## Discussion focus

Euthyphro's successive attempts to define piety and why Socrates finds
them inadequate; the Euthyphro Question ("is the pious loved by the gods
because it is pious, or pious because it is loved by the gods?") and its
analogue for monotheistic divine-command theories.
