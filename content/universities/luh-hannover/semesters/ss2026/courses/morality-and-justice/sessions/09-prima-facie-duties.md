# Session 9 (Jun 19): Prima Facie Duties; A Plurality of Moral Rules

## Required reading

- Ross, W. D., *What Makes Right Acts Right* (course textbook, ch. 11).

## Discussion focus

Ross's objections to consequentialism and to Kant's exceptionless moral
rules; what he means by "prima facie duties" as distinct from "duty
proper," and how they're meant to guide action in particular situations.
