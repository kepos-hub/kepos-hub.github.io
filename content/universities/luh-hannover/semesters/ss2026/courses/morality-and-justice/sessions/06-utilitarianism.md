# Session 6 (May 22): Utilitarianism

## Required reading

- Mill, John Stuart, *Utilitarianism* (course textbook, ch. 8).
- Williams, Bernard, *A Critique of Utilitarianism*.

## Discussion focus

Whether utilitarian impartiality really rules out partiality to oneself
or family; Mill's claim that virtuous people rarely act with the Greatest
Happiness Principle explicitly in mind; whether motive is really
irrelevant to an action's morality; Williams's objection to utilitarianism
and whether it's a serious problem for the view.
