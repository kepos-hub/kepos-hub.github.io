# Session 11 (Jul 3): Metaethics and Skepticism About Objective Morality

## Required reading

- Hume, David, *Moral Distinctions Not Derived From Reason* (course
  textbook, ch. 14).

## Optional reading

- Enoch, David, *Why I Am an Objectivist about Ethics (And Why You Are
  Too)* (course textbook, ch. 17).

## Discussion focus

Hume's claim that reason has no influence on our passions and actions,
and his argument that moral rules aren't conclusions of reason; whether
moral judgments are best explained as products of passion rather than
reason.
