# Session 7 (Jun 5): Kantian Ethics

## Required reading

- Kant, Immanuel, *The Good Will and the Categorical Imperative* (course
  textbook, ch. 9).

## Discussion focus

What Kant means by calling the good will "good without limitation";
his reasons for denying that happiness is always good; the distinction
between acting "in conformity with duty" and acting "from duty," and
whether only the latter has moral worth.
