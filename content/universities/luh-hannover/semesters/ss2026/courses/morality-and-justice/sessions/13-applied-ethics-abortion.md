# Session 13 (Jul 17): Applied Ethics; Abortion

## Required reading

- Thomson, Judith Jarvis, *A Defense of Abortion* (course textbook, ch.
  31).
- Marquis, Don, *Why Abortion Is Immoral* (course textbook, ch. 32).

## Discussion focus

Thomson's violinist thought experiment and what it does (and doesn't)
show about the permissibility of abortion; her account of what a "right to
life" amounts to; whether Marquis's criticisms of the standard arguments
on both sides succeed.
