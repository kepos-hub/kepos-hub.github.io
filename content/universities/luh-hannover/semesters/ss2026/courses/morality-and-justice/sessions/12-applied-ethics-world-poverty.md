# Session 12 (Jul 10): Applied Ethics; World Poverty

## Required reading

- Singer, Peter, *The Singer Solution to World Poverty* (course textbook,
  ch. 20).
- Brennan, Jason, *International Aid: Not the Cure You're Hoping For*
  (course textbook, ch. 22).

## Discussion focus

Singer's "Bob and the Bugatti" case and what it's meant to show about our
obligations to strangers vs. those we alone can save; whether the
difference between Bob's situation and everyday charity is morally
relevant; Brennan's institutional-change objection to aid-based solutions.
