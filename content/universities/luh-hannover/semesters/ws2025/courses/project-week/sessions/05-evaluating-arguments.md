# Session 5 (Nov 20): Evaluating Arguments

## Aim

Different argument forms and their weaknesses; what evidence would call an
argument's soundness into question; strategies for attacking, or
insulating, an argument.

## Pre-reading

- Saul, Jennifer (2012), "Just Go Ahead and Lie."

## Weekly exercise (1–2 pages, due before class)

Reconstruct one or more of Saul's arguments (what's her main thesis, and
how does she argue for it — including the positions she rejects), then
reflect on whether they succeed.

## Notes

Introduces the topic for the mini-essay (based on Saul's text).
