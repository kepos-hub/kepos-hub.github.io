# Session 7 (Dec 4): Structuring a Paper, Writing an Introduction

## Aim

What structure is and why it matters; how structure should reflect the
argumentative strategy at the level of the whole paper, section, and
paragraph; the simplest model structure for a philosophy essay.

## Weekly exercise (due Dec 4)

Analyze the introductions of the two papers found in session 6 (how do
they lead into the topic and capture interest?), then draft a
(preliminary) introduction for your own essay.
