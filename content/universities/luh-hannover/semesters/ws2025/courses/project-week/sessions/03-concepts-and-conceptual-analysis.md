# Session 3 (Nov 6, online): Concepts and Conceptual Analysis

## Aim

What concepts and conceptual analyses are, and how conceptual analysis
figures into philosophical arguments and critiques of them.

## Pre-reading

- Gettier, Edmund (1963), "Is Justified True Belief Knowledge?"

## Weekly exercise (due before class)

Read Gettier (1963) and: (1) identify the main question, (2) check whether
Gettier follows the "They Say / I Say" scheme and summarize accordingly,
(3) articulate one objection to Gettier's argument (or a plausible
objection someone else might raise).
