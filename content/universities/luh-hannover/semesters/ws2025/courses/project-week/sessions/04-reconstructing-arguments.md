# Session 4 (Nov 13): Reconstructing Arguments

## Aim

What arguments are and how they work; validity vs. soundness; the
importance of reconstructing informal arguments in formally valid form.

## Pre-reading

- Nelson, Lynn Hankinson (1995), "Feminist Epistemology."

## Weekly exercise

Answer, on Nelson (1995): (1) how she reconstructs the argument that the
very idea of a feminist epistemology is incongruent, (2) how she argues
against that idea, (3) summarize via "They Say / I Say."
