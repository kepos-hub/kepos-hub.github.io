# Chapter 3 — To Go Beyond

- **Narrative:** Measuring temperature when thermometers melt and freeze.
- **Analysis:** The extension of concepts beyond their birth domains.

## Discussion questions

- What is "operationalism," and how does it relate to the notion of
  "validity" (see "The Validation of Standards," pp. 40–44)?
- How does Chang confront the apparent impossibility of validating
  measurement methods under Bridgman's operationalism (think: Wittgenstein
  and "meaning as use," p. 150)?
- Does Chang's critique of Bridgman's operationalism extend to concepts
  outside the physical sciences (e.g. the social sciences)? Can you think of
  examples where it would, or wouldn't, hold?
