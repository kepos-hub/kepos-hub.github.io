# Chapter 2 — Spirit, Air, and Quicksilver

- **Narrative:** The search for the "real" scale of temperature.
- **Analysis:** Measurement theory in the context of empiricism.

## Discussion questions

- What is "the problem of nomic measurement"?
- Did Regnault's criterion of comparability (pp. 59–60, 77, 89) conclusively
  solve the (nomic) problem of temperature measurement, by showing which
  thermometric measurement operations are most internally consistent?
- Chang argues that Regnault's principle of single value "is not an
  empirical hypothesis" (p. 91) and "can only have a metaphysical
  justification" (p. 92). Does the argument succeed? Why or why not?
