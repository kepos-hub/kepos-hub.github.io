# Philosophy of Science (M.A.) — Leibniz University Hannover (LUH)

## Program facts

- **Degree:** Master of Arts (M.A.)
- **Language of instruction:** English
- **Standard duration:** 4 semesters
- **Faculty:** Faculty of Humanities
- **Admission:** Unrestricted (open to all qualified applicants)
- **Intake:** Winter and summer semester

## Admission requirements

An undergraduate degree in philosophy or a related field (physics, chemistry,
biology, astronomy, mathematics, political science, sociology, psychology,
historiography, or economics). A selection committee evaluates whether a
given degree qualifies.

## Application deadlines

| Applicant group | Winter semester | Summer semester |
| --- | --- | --- |
| EU / German students | Jun 1 – Jul 15 | Dec 1 – Jan 15 |
| Non-EU students (uni-assist / VPD) | Apr 15 – May 31 | Oct 15 – Nov 30 (previous year) |

## Program focus

Centers on critical reflection on the sciences, with focus areas in ethics
and epistemology of the natural sciences, life sciences, humanities, and
social sciences. Coursework spans metaphysics, epistemology, philosophy of
mind, and moral/political philosophy, plus elective modules in specialized
areas.
