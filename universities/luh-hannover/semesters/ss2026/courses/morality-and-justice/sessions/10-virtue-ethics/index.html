    Session 10 (Jun 26): Virtue Ethics¶

    Required reading¶

    • Aristotle, Nicomachean Ethics (course textbook, ch. 12).

    Discussion focus¶

    Aristotle's distinction between performing virtuous actions and being a virtuous person; virtue as a "mean" between extremes and whether that's consistent with holding some acts always wrong; his advice for finding the mean.
