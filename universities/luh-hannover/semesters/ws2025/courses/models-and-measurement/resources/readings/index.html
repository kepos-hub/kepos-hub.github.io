    Reading list — Models and Measurement¶

    Required¶

    • Chang, Hasok (2004), Inventing Temperature: Measurement and Scientific Progress. Oxford University Press.

    General introductions¶

    • Hand, D. J. (2016), Measurement: A Very Short Introduction. Oxford University Press.
    • Tal, Eran (Fall 2020 ed.), "Measurement in Science," The Stanford Encyclopedia of Philosophy, Edward N. Zalta (ed.), https://plato.stanford.edu/archives/fall2020/entries/measurement-science/.

    Optional — by topic¶

    All optional; useful for a term paper or for going deeper on a chapter.

    Metrology (intergovernmental)¶

    • BIPM (2012), International Vocabulary of Metrology: Basic and General Concepts and Associated Terms, JCGM 200:2012. doi:10.59161/JCGM200-2012
    • BIPM (2019), The International System of Units. doi:10.59161/AUEZ1291
    • BIPM (2023), Guide to the Expression of Uncertainty in Measurement, Part 1: Introduction, JCGM GUM-1:2023. doi:10.59161/JCGMGUM-1-2023

    Representational theory of measurement (RTM)¶

    • Baccelli, J. (2020), "Beyond the Metrological Viewpoint," Studies in History and Philosophy of Science Part A 80, 56–61. [defense]
    • Tal, E. (2021), "Two Myths of Representational Measurement," Perspectives on Science 29(6), 701–741. [critical]

    Measurement as evidence¶

    • Koyré, A. (1953), "An Experiment in Measurement," Proceedings of the American Philosophical Society 97(2), 222–237.
    • Smith, G. E. (2002), "The Methodology of the Principia," in The Cambridge Companion to Newton, 138–173.
    • Tal, E. (2016), "How Does Measuring Generate Evidence? The Problem of Observational Grounding," Journal of Physics: Conference Series 772. doi:10.1088/1742-6596/772/1/012001

    Modeling measurement¶

    • Tal, E. (2011), "How Accurate Is the Standard Second?" Philosophy of Science 78, 1082–1096.
    • Giordani, A. & Mari, L. (2012), "Measurement, Models, and Uncertainty," IEEE Transactions on Instrumentation and Measurement 61, 2144–2152.
    • Tal, E. (2013), "Old and New Problems in Philosophy of Measurement," Philosophy Compass 8, 1159–1173.
    • Tal, E. (2017), "Calibration: Modelling the Measurement Process," Studies in History and Philosophy of Science Part A 65, 33–45.
    • Tal, E. (2019), "Individuating Quantities," Philosophical Studies 176(4), 853–878.
    • Mari, L., Carbone, P., Giordani, A., & Petri, D. (2017), "A Structural Interpretation of Measurement and Some Related Epistemological Issues," Studies in History and Philosophy of Science Part A 65, 46–56.

    Measurement and statistics¶

    • Larroulet Philippi, C. (2022), "On Measurement Scales: Neither Ordinal Nor Interval?"
    • Larroulet Philippi, C. (2022), "Against Prohibition (Or, When Using Ordinal Scales to Compare Groups Is OK)."
    • Mayo-Wilson, C. (forthcoming), "Measured Inference: Scales, Statistics, and Scientific Inference."

    Measurement across the sciences¶

    • Mari, L., Wilson, M., & Maul, A. (2023), Measurement Across the Sciences: Developing a Shared Concept System for Measurement. Springer Nature.

    Measurement in psychology¶

    • Feest, U. (2005), "Operationism in Psychology: What the Debate Is About, What the Debate Should Be About," Journal of the History of the Behavioral Sciences 41(2), 131–149.
    • Sherry, D. (2011), "Thermoscopes, Thermometers, and the Foundations of Measurement," Studies in History and Philosophy of Science Part A 42(4), 509–524.
    • Michell, J. (2014), An Introduction to the Logic of Psychological Measurement. Psychology Press. [accessible, critical intro to RTM]
    • Vessonen, E. (2020), "The Complementarity of Psychometrics and the Representational Theory of Measurement," BJPS.
    • Duque, S. R., Tal, E., & Barbic, S. P. (2024), "The Role of Ethical and Social Values in Psychosocial Measurement," Measurement 225, 113993.
    • Montero-Espinoza, D. (2025), "Rethinking Psychiatric Symptoms: The Role of Measurement Heterogeneity in Diagnostic Validity," History and Philosophy of the Life Sciences 47(2), 20.

    Measurement in the human sciences¶

    • Barwich, A. S. & Chang, H. (2015), "Sensory Measurements: Coordination and Standardization," Biological Theory 10(3), 200–211.
    • Alexandrova, A. (2017), "Is Well-being Measurable After All?" Public Health Ethics 10(2), 129–137.
    • Larroulet Philippi, C. (2024), "Is Quantitative Measurement in the Human Sciences Doomed? On the Quantity Objection," BJPS.

    Measurement in paleoclimatology¶

    • Bokulich, A. (2020), "Calibration, Coherence, and Consilience in Radiometric Measures of Geologic Time," Philosophy of Science 87, 425–456.
    • Wilson, J. & Boudinot, F. G. (2022), "Proxy Measurement in Paleoclimatology," European Journal for Philosophy of Science 12. doi:10.1007/s13194-021-00444-8
    • Watkins, A. (2024), "Paleoclimate Proxies and the Benefits of Disunity," Philosophy of Science 91, 793–810.

    Measurement in climate science¶

    • Dethier, C. (2022), "Calibrating Statistical Tools: Improving the Measure of Humanity's Influence on the Climate," Studies in History and Philosophy of Science 94, 158–166.
    • Dethier, C. (forthcoming), "Stability, Robustness Reasoning, and Measuring the Human Contribution to Warming," Philosophy of Science. doi:10.1017/psa.2025.10135
    • Ackermann, M. (forthcoming), "Climate Sensitivity through the Lens of Measurement Practice," BJPS. doi:10.1086/738572

    Measurement and computer simulation¶

    • Morrison, M. (2009), "Models, Measurement, and Computer Simulation: The Changing Face of Experimentation," Philosophical Studies 143, 33–57.
    • Parker, W. S. (2017), "Computer Simulation, Measurement, and Data Assimilation," BJPS 68, 273–304.
